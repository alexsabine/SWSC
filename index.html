The Harvest Moon

The Somewhen Survival Society invites new members to join its growing ranks this September and October.

Gather beneath the Harvest Moon in village halls across the Isle of Wight as we share foraged food, stories, songs and the Society's latest discoveries from the Somewhen.

Part performance, part feast, part gathering, these intimate evenings offer an invitation into an unfolding piece of Island folklore. Guided by members of the Society, audiences will uncover fragments of a hidden history, encounter curious traditions, and learn the survival skills passed down by those who have ventured closest to the Somewhen.

As the evening unfolds, the boundary between our world and the myth of the Somewhen begins to blur.

This unique theatrical experience invites you to step inside a living mythology and discover what it takes to survive the Somewhen.

About Can You Hear Us?

Can You Hear Us? is a six-week Island-wide celebration of culture and creativity, taking place across the Isle of Wight in Autumn 2026. Led by Creative Island, the project aims to showcase local talent, strengthen community arts, and connect the Island's heritage and environment to the wider world.
